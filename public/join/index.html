Time with the Father

Reader community

You don't have to read through the Bible alone. This is a place for people working through Time with the Father to share what they're finding, ask questions, pray for each other, and keep going.

Opening soon The community is being set up now. Check back here; this page will have the link to join.

What it will have

  • A shared space to share your notes and what stuck with you, ask questions, and post prayer requests.
  • Small accountability groups of three, matched with people at about the same point in the reading plan.
  • Milestones worth celebrating together, like finishing the first five books and finishing the whole Bible.

Whatever day you're on

The journal is undated on purpose. Whether you're on Day 1 or Day 200, and whether this is your first time through or your tenth, you'll be welcome.
